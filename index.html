About

I'm Amirhosein Eftekhari, a creative director working between Kuwait and Iran. I started in 2D animation and motion design, and today most of my work mixes AI and CGI footage with hand-made animation, compositing and sound design.

That mix lets a brand put a giant graduate in front of the Kuwait Towers, crash a plane into a billboard, or tell a fully illustrated story without a full production crew, while still looking crafted rather than generated.

I write the concept, direct the look, generate or animate the shots, and finish the edit and sound myself.
